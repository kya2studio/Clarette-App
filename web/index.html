Your headshot workflow

  1. Import a ZIP or images. The source pixels are kept intact.
  2. Enhance if needed. Detail only preserves source dimensions and existing masks. Auto + detail upscales first when the output framing requires it. Inspect faces at 100%.
  3. Position with Auto Fit, Head Box, or Move. Adjust color, then click Apply Color.
  4. Detect subject. Red means keep. Brush Keep or Remove for corrections; Refine hair estimates fine transparency near an edge.
  5. Use Compare to check the cutout. Click Apply cutout when ready.
  6. Choose your Final folder and batch subfolder, then Save batch finals. This exports every existing cutout in the active batch, offering to apply pending edits first. Images without masks stay in the batch. Clear saved images removes verified temporary copies only.

Use Photoshop for other edits. Save to the same Working file; Clarette checks for changes. External edits reset color and masks for review.

Close and reopen to resume. Originals, full-resolution masks, color settings and framing are saved locally. The 300% loupe inspects source pixels; it does not change your export size.
